# Functional Requirements

## Essential

1. Support two cameras.
2. Detect movement.
3. Capture an image when activity is detected.
4. Detect faces.
5. Recognize registered users.
6. Record attendance.
7. Store date and time.
8. Store unknown-face events.
9. Display attendance history.
10. Manage registered users.
11. Provide a web/app dashboard.

## Optional

1. Liveness detection.
2. Excel/PDF export.
3. Attendance statistics.
4. Multiple classrooms.

## Out of Scope (Not necessary, pi4 4gb cant handle)

1. Continuous CCTV surveillance.
2. 24/7 cloud video recording.
3. Large-scale enterprise deployment.
4. High-end real-time video analytics.
