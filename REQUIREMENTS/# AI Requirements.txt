# AI Requirements

## Face Detection

Preferred model:
SCRFD/Deepface

Purpose:
Locate faces within captured images.

## Face Recognition

Preferred framework:
InsightFace

Recognition model:
ArcFace

Purpose:
Generate face embeddings and compare them against registered users.

## Important Design Principle

Do not use YOLO for continuous person detection unless testing
demonstrates that it is necessary.

Motion detection should preferably be handled using lightweight
OpenCV techniques.

AI inference should occur only after relevant activity is detected.

The final model should be selected based on actual Raspberry Pi 4
performance testing rather than assumptions.
